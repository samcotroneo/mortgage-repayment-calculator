# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Australian owner-occupiers who actively manage a mortgage and one or more offset accounts.

## Product Purpose

Ledge is a mortgage-first financial cockpit. It helps people understand how their loan, offset balances, repayments, and everyday cash flow combine to shape their payoff forecast.

## Positioning

The product starts with the user's own statement files instead of requiring bank credentials or an expensive aggregation subscription. Forecasts show their source, freshness, assumptions, and relationship to attached accounts.

## Operating Context

People use the product during regular household money check-ins, after importing a bank statement, when rates or repayments change, and when considering extra repayments or refinancing scenarios.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mortgage and loan-split modelling with multiple offset accounts.
- Linked account visibility and transaction expense tracking.
- Privacy-first CSV, OFX, and QIF statement imports with review and correction.
- Explainable payoff forecasts and companion calculators.
- PDF extraction and automated bank feeds are deliberately deferred or experimental.
- Forecasts are educational projections, not financial advice or lender-verified balances.

## Brand Commitments

- Working product name: Ledge.
- Voice: clear, grounded, quietly confident, and explicit about uncertainty.
- Never require bank credentials to deliver first value.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains an early mortgage repayment calculator with offset-account modelling. There is no production bank connection, account system, or verified lender data source yet.

## Product Principles

- Make the mortgage relationship legible.
- Treat imported data as reviewable evidence, not unquestionable truth.
- Show the effect of small changes without hiding assumptions.
- Earn trust through freshness, provenance, and reversible actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience should remain keyboard usable, responsive on small screens, readable at comfortable zoom levels, and clear without relying on colour alone.
